Searches the last 24 hours of X (Twitter) posts about a topic you pick and delivers at most five worth your time, each with why it matters and a suggested action. Use it to find customers, watch competitors, or follow anything you're curious about.

## Deploy

Give this prompt to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any other coding agent. It walks you through setup, creates the agent in your Omnara project, and runs it once.

```text
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omnara-ai/omnara/main/examples/x-signal-agent/SKILL.md and follow it to deploy the X signal agent.
```

To see how it works or adapt it, browse [`examples/x-signal-agent`](https://github.com/omnara-ai/omnara/tree/main/examples/x-signal-agent) on GitHub.
