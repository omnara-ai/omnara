Scrapes the last 24 hours of LinkedIn posts matching your searches, plus new posts from the companies and people you track, and delivers at most five worth your time. It uses Apify's hosted MCP server and needs no LinkedIn account or cookie.

## Deploy

Give this prompt to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any other coding agent. It walks you through setup, creates the agent in your Omnara project, and runs it once.

```text
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/omnara-ai/omnara/main/examples/linkedin-signal-agent/SKILL.md and follow it to deploy the LinkedIn signal agent.
```

To see how it works or adapt it, browse [`examples/linkedin-signal-agent`](https://github.com/omnara-ai/omnara/tree/main/examples/linkedin-signal-agent) on GitHub.
