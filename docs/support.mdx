- **Email** — [contact@omnara.com](mailto:contact@omnara.com) for anything: questions, bug reports, account or billing issues, feedback.
- **Discord** — [join the community](https://discord.gg/Dc46sYk6e3) to ask questions, share what you're building, and follow release announcements.
- **Slack** — teams and enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel with the Omnara team; ask over email if yours isn't set up yet.

Wherever you reach out, include public IDs (`agt_…`, `org_…`) for anything specific — they're safe to share and make investigation much faster.
